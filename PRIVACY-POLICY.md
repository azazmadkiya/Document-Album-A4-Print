# Privacy Policy - A4 Document Studio

**Last updated:** September 26, 2026

**A4 Document Studio** ("we", "our", or "us") built the A4 Document Studio app as an offline-first utility application. This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Our app operates on a strict **local-first** principle:
- All scanned documents, ID cards (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Driving License, Student ID), captured images, and generated A4 PDF files are stored securely and exclusively on your device's local storage using a local Room Database.
- We do **not** collect, upload, store, or transmit any of your personal documents, scans, or photos to any external servers or cloud databases.

## 2. Camera and Storage Permissions
- **Camera:** Requested strictly to allow you to photograph or scan your physical identity cards and documents.
- **Storage / Media:** Requested solely to allow you to pick existing gallery images or save generated A4 PDF documents to your device. 
- All processed images and generated PDFs remain 100% on your device.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks that collect personally identifiable information.

## 4. Security
Because all data remains locally on your device, your document security is backed by your device's built-in operating system security measures and optional PDF password protection features.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
