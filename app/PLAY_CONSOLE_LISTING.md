## Full Description (Up to 4,000 characters)

**A4 Document Studio** is your ultimate offline-first utility application designed to effortlessly scan, crop, arrange, and format multiple identity cards and documents onto professional A4-sized sheets. Whether you need to print your Aadhaar card, PAN card, Voter ID, Driving License, or student ID cards (Front and Back aligned), A4 Document Studio makes the process quick, clean, and professional.

### 🌟 Key Features:

- **Smart ID Card & Document Scanner:** Instantly photograph and capture physical identity cards and documents with built-in edge detection and cropping tools.
- **A4 Layout Management:** Easily combine multiple front and back card scans or multi-page documents onto a single A4 canvas for organized printing or sharing.
- **PDF Password Protection:** Secure your exported PDF files with custom encryption and passwords before sharing or printing.
- **Image Enhancement Filters:** Apply Black & White, Grayscale, or Color filters, and adjust contrast to ensure crisp, legible scans.
- **100% Offline & Private:** Powered by a local Room Database. All your documents, scans, and PDFs remain entirely on your device with zero cloud uploads or data tracking.
- **High-Resolution PDF Export:** Generate professional A4-grade PDFs ready for wireless printing or sharing via email and messaging apps.

### 🔒 Privacy & Security:
We respect your personal data. A4 Document Studio requires camera and storage permissions strictly for local document scanning and saving PDFs to your device. No personal data is ever collected or transmitted to external servers.
