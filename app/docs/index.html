Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Document Album A4 Print ("we", "our", or "us") built the Document Album A4 Print app as an offline-first utility app. This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our app operates on a local-first principle. All documents, images, scans, and PDFs you capture, import, edit, or generate are stored securely and exclusively on your device's local storage using a local Room Database.

We do not collect, upload, store, or transmit any of your personal documents, ID cards, scans, or photos to any external servers or cloud databases.

2. Camera and Storage Permissions

The app requests camera access strictly to allow you to scan or photograph your documents and ID cards. It requests storage/media permissions solely to let you pick existing photos or save generated A4 PDF documents to your device. This data never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks that collect personally identifiable information.

4. Security

Because all data remains locally on your device, your document security is backed by your device's built-in operating system security measures.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
