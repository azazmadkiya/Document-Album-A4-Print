# Privacy Policy for Document Album A4 Print

**Effective Date:** September 26, 2026

Document Album A4 Print ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Document Album A4 Print mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our application.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Our app is designed with your privacy in mind. **All document scanning, image cropping, edge detection, and A4 page composition happen entirely on your device locally.** 

- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, images, documents, or metadata on external servers.
- **Local Storage:** All saved documents, cropped ID card scans, and A4 PDFs are stored exclusively in your device's local app-specific storage using Room Database and app cache directories.

## 2. Camera and Storage Permissions
- **Camera:** Used solely for capturing photos of ID cards and documents within the app for cropping and A4 layout generation. Images captured are processed on-device and are never uploaded to any remote server.
- **Storage / Media:** Used to select existing photos or save generated A4 PDF documents to your device.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Our application does not use external analytics trackers, advertising networks, or third-party data collection SDKs.

## 4. Security
We value your trust in providing us your local document data, thus we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it locally on your device. However, remember that no method of electronic storage on mobile devices is 100% secure and reliable.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
